  • Delivery — we bring the scooter to your hotel in the city centre for rentals of 2 days or more

Tips

  • Ask reception where to park the scooter safely.
  • Hotels 1–7 are a short walk from the night market and Xuan Huong Lake.
  • Roads around Tuyen Lam Lake are dark at night — ride back before dark.
  • Star ratings and prices change — check with the hotel when you book.
