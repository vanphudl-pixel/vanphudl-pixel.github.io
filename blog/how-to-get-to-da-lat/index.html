How to Get to Da Lat from Nha Trang, Ho Chi Minh City and Mui Ne

Da Lat is in the mountains and there's no railway from the big cities. People arrive by bus, minivan or plane — here's what's most convenient.

Updated: Oct 16, 2026

From Nha Trang

A very popular route. It's about 135–140 km along a scenic mountain road over a pass, taking roughly 3.5–4 hours.

  • Bus — several departures a day from different companies, for example Futa (Phương Trang).
  • Limousine van — small minibuses with comfortable seats that often pick you up from your hotel.

Tip: the road is winding — if you get carsick, sit near the front and don't eat a heavy meal before the trip.

From Ho Chi Minh City

About 300 km.

  • Overnight sleeper bus — roughly 6–8 hours, and you save a night at a hotel.
  • Plane — about an hour to Lien Khuong Airport. From the airport to central Da Lat is about 30 km by taxi or shuttle.

From Mui Ne

About 160 km, roughly 4 hours by bus or minivan. The road climbs from the sea into the mountains and it gets noticeably cooler — keep a sweater handy.

Where to buy tickets

Bus tickets are sold at bus company offices, in apps and at most hotels and travel agencies. On holidays and during Tết tickets sell out fast — book ahead.

Your bike on arrival

Buses usually arrive at the bus station or company offices in Da Lat. If you rent a bike for 2 days or more, we'll deliver it free to your hotel in the center — no need to go anywhere. Just message us your arrival date and hotel name in advance.
