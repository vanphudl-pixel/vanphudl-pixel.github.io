Electric or gas scooter in Da Lat?

Electric and gas scooters both have pros and cons. This guide helps you choose the right bike for your plans.

Quick comparison

  • License: electric scooters need none; gas scooters need a valid motorbike license.
  • Range: electric scooters are estimated at about 50–60 km per full charge, depending on the road and riding style. Gas scooters go further on one tank.
  • Charging and battery swap: swap the battery at the shop for 20,000 VND each time.
  • Load limit: electric scooters carry up to 130 kg.
  • Noise: electric scooters are quieter and smoother.

When to choose electric

Pick an electric scooter if you mostly ride around town, go to cafés and the night market, don't have a license, or want a quiet ride.

When to choose gas

Pick a gas scooter if you plan longer rides in the day, go up many hills, or don't want to think about battery range. The 125cc Honda Air Blade has a larger engine than the 110cc Honda Vision, so it feels more comfortable on climbs.

Tip

Whichever you choose, check your load and your route. If you're not sure, message the shop with your plans and we'll advise you.

Frequently asked questions

How far can an electric scooter go on one charge?

About 50–60 km, but the real distance depends on the road, the load and how you ride. If you need more, swap the battery at the shop for 20,000 VND each time.

Is an electric or gas scooter cheaper?

Both cost 150,000 VND per day. The differences are the license requirement, range and how you recharge.
