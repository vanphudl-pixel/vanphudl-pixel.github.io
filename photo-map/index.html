Seasons worth planning for

  • Wild sunflowers (dã quỳ) — late Oct – Dec
  • Pink grass hills (cỏ hồng) — Nov – Dec
  • Cherry blossom (mai anh đào) — late Jan – Feb
  • Cloud hunting — early mornings after a rainy night

Photo & riding tips

  • Golden light: 5:30–7:30 and 16:00–17:30.
  • Mornings are cold — bring a jacket.
  • Never stop on a bend for a photo; pull fully off the road.
  • Vietnam drives on the right. Helmets always on.
